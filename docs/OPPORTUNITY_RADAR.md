# Daily opportunity radar

Search at least 20 distinct accessible websites each morning. Prioritize direct open submissions, judged cash-prize creative contests, brand advertising challenges, fixed-pay production briefs and recurring paid video contracts. Freelance applications are secondary. Record actual sources checked, blocked, and replacements; do not count duplicate pages as independent sites.

## Hard filters
- Canada-based or worldwide participation; audience geography is not creator residency.
- AI-generated or programmatically produced content explicitly allowed; if unclear, list as unverified, not qualified.
- 5–45 seconds per finished deliverable; multiple separate clips acceptable.
- No established social following, minimum views, engagement-based judging or view-based pay.
- No actual gameplay recording, self-filming, mandatory possession/purchase of physical products.
- No new paid tools, subscriptions, entry fees, required credit purchases or paid memberships. Free registration is fine. Existing OpenArt and Grok access does not imply unlimited credits.
- Direct upload is preferred. Posting to X or a new account is acceptable only if audience metrics are irrelevant.
- Avoid scams, restricted-product promotions, exploitative unpaid custom auditions and unclear rights. Voluntary judged contests are distinct from guaranteed paid work.

## Source registry (starting candidates, not verified current openings)
Content Rewards (fixed fee only), Tongal, eYeka, Freelancer Contests, FilmFreeway, Festhome, Shortfilmdepot, AI Film Contests, AIFC, Idea.ai, official Runway, Pika, Luma AI, Kling AI, OpenArt and Higgsfield challenges, Upwork, Contra, PeoplePerHour, Guru, X, Reddit, official brand/agency open calls. Discover new sources; replace inactive or inaccessible sites. Only include free-entry opportunities with compatible tools; a free tool-specific contest may be considered separately.

## Report contract
For each actual opportunity: official link and verification date, brand/product, payment currency and amount, contracted vs competitive prize, deadline/timezone, residency, AI and tool rules, duration/aspect ratio, required assets/text/audio/disclosures, submission process, usage rights, cost/effort estimate if supportable, creative starting point, blocker and next action. Rank by fit and expected return, not total prize pool. Distinguish selected prize, approved deliverable payment and advertised budget. Never equate AI permission with the right to use protected assets.

Provide 3–10 qualified opportunities if available, otherwise say none. Label unverified leads separately; deduplicate against prior reports where possible. No automatic applications or submissions.
